    Cast from your phone, or open /tv in any TV or laptop browser.

  2. Everyone joins

    Friends scan the QR code on the TV. Empty seats are played by the computer.

  3. Swing

    Flick up to serve, swing to hit, roll your wrist for spin. Phones without motion sensors can swipe.
